# Content policy page — draft copy

For the public web page (`/content-policy`) and a matching in-app link. Do not
publish until the launch position in [CONTENT-RIGHTS.md](CONTENT-RIGHTS.md) is
chosen; the wording must match what the product actually does.

## What SLNews publishes

SLNews is a reader that helps you find news from Sierra Leonean and
international publishers. For most stories we show a headline, a short excerpt
and a link to the publisher's own article. Where a publisher has given us
permission, we may show more of the story and its image inside the app.

We always name the publisher, keep the byline where the feed provides it, and
link to the original article.

## What we do not do

- We do not bypass paywalls, registration walls or other access controls.
- We do not claim ownership of publisher content.
- We do not alter the meaning of a headline or excerpt.
- We do not rehost publisher images on our own storage.

## Attribution and corrections

If a byline, source or link is wrong, tell us and we will correct it. If you are
a publisher and want a different credit format, we will use it.

## Removal requests

If you are a publisher or rights holder and want a story, image or excerpt
removed:

Email **content@[domain]** with the URL in SLNews, the original URL, and the
action you want. We aim to acknowledge within **2 working days** and to remove
or restrict the content within **48 hours**. We keep a suppression record so the
same URL is not re-ingested.

## Copyright complaints

If you believe content in SLNews infringes your copyright, send the work, the
URL in SLNews, your contact details, and a statement that you are authorised to
act for the rights holder. We will review and respond, and repeat infringers'
sources will be removed from ingestion.
