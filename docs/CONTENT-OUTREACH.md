# Content permission outreach kit

Companion to [CONTENT-RIGHTS.md](CONTENT-RIGHTS.md). The goal of the first email
is not a contract — it is a reply. A short written "yes, link to us and use our
headline and first paragraph" is enough to move a source from `link` to
`excerpt`; full text needs a signed note.

worse than a slow one.

## Email template (English)

Subject: **SLNews app — permission to show your headlines and first paragraph**

> Dear [Editor],
>
> I am [name], [role] at SLNews, a free Sierra Leonean news app for Android built
> by [company/team]. We are preparing to launch and want to do right by the
> publishers whose work we point readers to.
>
> Today SLNews shows a headline, a short excerpt and a link that opens your
> article on your site. [If applicable: We would also like to show your story
> image and the first two paragraphs inside the app, always with your byline and
> a link back to the full story.]
>
> Could you confirm in writing whether we may:
>
> 1. use your headlines and a short excerpt (up to 280 characters) with a link;
> 2. [optional] use one story image, credited to you;
> 3. [optional] show the first two paragraphs in the app.
>
> We will always name you as the source, link to the original article, never
> bypass a paywall or registration wall, and remove any of your content within
> 48 hours if you ask us to. We are also happy to share which stories readers
> open and how much traffic we send you.
>
> If it is easier, a one-line reply confirming what is acceptable is enough to
> start. I can also send a short one-page agreement if your team prefers.
>
> Thank you,
> [name] · [phone] · [email] · [website]

## Follow-up (after 5 working days)

> Following up on my note about SLNews. We are launching with link-out only, so
> nothing of yours is reproduced in full; I would still value your confirmation
> on headlines and excerpts, and I am happy to start with that if full text is
> not something you want to discuss yet.

## Call notes

- Lead with traffic and attribution, not "we need content".
- Ask what **they** want: credit format, link target, analytics, a partner page,
  an early look at a publisher dashboard.
- If they say "link only", record it as `link`; do not push for body text.
- If they ask about money, stop and escalate — do not improvise a revenue share.
- Record the exact wording of what they agreed to; screenshots of an email are
  not a licence, but the words are the record.

## What "yes" changes

- `link` → `excerpt`: headline + ≤ 280 chars + link (no body, no image).
- `excerpt` → `licensed`: full paragraphs and/or publisher image, with the
  agreed credit line and a revocation path.
- Any publisher can move back to `link` or `removed` at any time; the register
  records the date and the reason.
